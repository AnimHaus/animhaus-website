# AnimHaus Studios

> AnimHaus Studios is a high-octane animation house and visual engineering studio based in Kolkata, India. We specialise in cinematic storytelling through anime, manga, comics, cartoons, games, web systems, and digital marketing.

## About

AnimHaus Studios (also known as Anim Haus, Anime Haus, Animation House) is a creative collective founded and based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India. We blend Japanese animation aesthetics with Indian narrative traditions to create emotionally resonant, visually engineered work for global audiences.

Our core belief: every frame is a story, every story is a world.

## Services

- **Anime Production** — Original series, OVAs, music videos, and short films. Emotionally driven and cinematically crafted for global audiences.
- **Manga & Sequential Art** — Original manga, webtoons, one-shots, graphic novels, and character concept art. Panel-perfect storytelling.
- **Comics** — Western-format comics, graphic novel illustration, and storyboard production.
- **Cartoons** — 2D animated cartoons, character design, rigging, and motion graphics.
- **Games** — Game art, concept design, UI/UX for games, and interactive narrative design.
- **Web Systems** — High-performance websites, web apps, and digital product design using Next.js, React, and modern web technologies.
- **Digital Marketing** — Brand strategy, social media content, motion graphics for campaigns, and performance creatives.
