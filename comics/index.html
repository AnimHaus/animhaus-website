How We Work

From Script
to Print.

Comics are a craft of collaboration between writer and artist. Our process keeps both creative tracks in sync from day one.

Script & Issue Plan

Full issue scripts, breakdowns, and arc planning. Page count, beat sheets, and visual directions established here.

Pencils & Layouts

Rough page layouts approved before tight pencilling begins. Character model sheets locked at this stage.

Inks & Colour

Clean inks followed by full digital colouring — flat colour, rendering, and final lighting pass for maximum visual impact.

Lettering & Final Output

Professional balloon and caption placement, SFX design, and print-ready export for self-publishing or press submission.

Art Direction

Visual Language
& Style Range.

Comics are defined by their visual voice. We work across a full range of aesthetics — each matched to the tone of the story.

Superhero Blockbuster

Big, bold linework with dynamic staging and saturated colour — the visual language of the genre done right.

Literary & Painterly

Softer illustration and muted palette — suited for graphic memoirs, literary adaptations, and prestige fiction.

Indie & Underground

Loose, expressive, intentionally raw — for alt-comics, zines, and creator-owned experimental work.

Clean Digital & Flat

Contemporary flat digital style — sharp outlines, clean fills, and strong graphic design sensibility for webcomics.
