Style &
Aesthetic Range.

Our artists work across the full spectrum of manga visual language — from highly detailed realism to expressive loose linework.

Classic Manga B&W

Traditional ink and screentone manga — clean, high-contrast, and suited for print and digital publication.

Full Colour Webtoon

Vibrant digital colour styles for webtoon platforms — flat, cel-shaded, or painterly depending on the genre.

Painterly & Expressive

Loose, textured, illustrative work with a strong artistic presence — ideal for prestige or art-book production.

Manhwa / Korean Style

Clean lines, modern proportions, and webtoon-native formatting — built for vertical scroll platforms.
